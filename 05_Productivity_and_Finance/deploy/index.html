⏰ Daily 12:00 AM (Midnight) Routine Scan

A background routine scan is configured to trigger every night at 12:00 AM (00:00). It checks all project directories under g:\My Drive\Antigravity\Projects for newly added, modified, or deleted files. Changes are logged to scan_log.txt and the PROJECT_CATALOG.json and PROJECT_STATUS.md documents are refreshed automatically.

To register or verify the Windows Scheduled Task anytime, run: .\Setup-MidnightTask.ps1 in PowerShell.
