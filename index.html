Why Were You Having Trouble Locating Projects in Antigravity?

Antigravity was only viewing this subfolder (index folder) instead of your root projects directory. Because of that, only this folder was visible in your sidebar explorer!

To view all your projects simultaneously in Antigravity:
1. Go to File → Open Workspace from File... → Choose G:\My Drive\Antigravity\Projects\Projects.code-workspace.
2. Or click File → Open Folder... → Select G:\My Drive\Antigravity\Projects. All 60+ projects will immediately appear in your sidebar!
